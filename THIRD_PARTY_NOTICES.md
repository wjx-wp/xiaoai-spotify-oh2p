`patches/librespot-local-control.patch` follows the librespot MIT license.

Exact license texts available for redistributed patch targets and Android dependencies are stored in `LICENSES/`. When redistributing compiled binaries, review all transitive dependency notices generated by the corresponding build and include them; the source repository's notice set is not a substitute for a binary-distribution audit.
